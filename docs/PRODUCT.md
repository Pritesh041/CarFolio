# Carfolio

## Vision

Carfolio is a digital home for die-cast and scale-model car collectors.

It allows collectors to:

- Track what they own
- Record purchase information
- Estimate collection value
- Organize collections
- Create public showcases
- Manage wishlists
- Trade models
- Sell models
- Discover other collectors

## Core Philosophy

Carfolio should feel like a digital garage, not an inventory management application.

The product should make users feel proud of their collection.

## Target Users

### Casual Collector
Owns 20–100 models.

### Serious Collector
Owns 100–1,000+ models.

### Reseller / Trader
Frequently buys, sells, and trades.

### Showcase Collector
Mostly wants to display their collection publicly.

## Initial Scope

Focus primarily on die-cast cars.

Support:

- Hot Wheels
- Matchbox
